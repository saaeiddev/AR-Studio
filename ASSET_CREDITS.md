# Asset sources and limitations

Models are fetched from their providers at runtime and are not stored in this repository. Internet access is required. Pins for GitHub-backed assets are in `library.js`.

* Kenney 3D model packs, distributed by Hidencod/tge-assets, CC0 1.0: https://github.com/Hidencod/tge-assets and https://kenney.nl/assets. These are primarily stylized low-poly models, not photorealistic models.
* Interactive Brain Project model and associated descriptive metadata, CC BY-SA 4.0: Z-Anatomy contributors and BodyParts3D / Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS). Additional registered atlas sources and detailed attribution: https://github.com/itayinbarr/brainproject/blob/2929e94f521a8ddceab26bc100a98dc06b0da060/README.md and https://github.com/itayinbarr/brainproject/blob/2929e94f521a8ddceab26bc100a98dc06b0da060/LICENSE. It includes approximate image-registered structures and is educational, not for clinical use. Descriptions are available for a subset of structures only. No medical review has been performed by this project.
* Four-cylinder engine and transmission stand, CC0: https://3dassets.dev/assets/car-factory-production-line-engine-and-transmission-st-eacd58df. Static stylized asset, not an animated cutaway; it does not have verified detailed part descriptions.
* Circuit board, CC0: https://3dassets.dev/assets/scavengers-loot-library-circuit-board-8b1aed98. Static stylized prop, not a detailed simulation.

Assets and attribution need to remain available in downstream distributed projects. Brain model and associated metadata are CC BY-SA 4.0; the code's license does not supersede the models' respective licenses. External servers may change or become unavailable. Do not represent these as offline bundled assets.
